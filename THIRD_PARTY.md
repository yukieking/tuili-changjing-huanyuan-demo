# Third-party components

The frontend bundles Three.js 0.180.0 and its OrbitControls addon from the official npm package `three`.

Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r180

License: MIT, retained at `src/main/resources/static/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`.

Only the browser modules required for this demo are vendored. No CDN requests are required at runtime.

The novel title and character/background references are content references, not covered by the Three.js license. The spatial model is an editorial concept reconstruction and is not an authorized or verified novel floor plan.
